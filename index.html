No. 9 · Homepage directions Internal · For Mike

Three takes on the homepage,
all whispering, none shouting.

Each pulls from the KCD restraint pattern: hero says almost nothing, services live one screen down, no clients listed. Same nine services (mapped 01–09 because the name asks for it), three different temperatures. Click any one to view.

Option A
The Wordmark
The most KCD-faithful. Cream paper, single oversized "No.9", italic tagline, two-column service index below. Calm, confident, very little to read.
Open A→
Option B
The Manifesto
Dark mode, editorial weight. "We — build worlds. / make news. / move markets." rotates in the hero. Services as a big-type list with hover lift.
Open B→
Option C
The Masthead
Treats the homepage like a magazine cover. Vol., issue, masthead, then a Table of Contents grid where each service is a "department." Worldbuilding made literal.
Open C→

A — best when…

You want the safest, most KCD-like read. Lets the wordmark do all the work; clients will fill the white space with their own associations.

B — best when…

You want the homepage to assert a point of view. The rotating verbs do the talking the wordmark can't, without a paragraph of copy.

C — best when…

You want world-building baked into the design system itself. Strongest narrative, most ownable, slightly more produced.
